About us

We are a group of passionate students from ENSIA, dedicated to turning brilliant ideas into reality. by creating a platform where learning and sharing skills is free, fun, and accessible to everyone.

Our Story

As future AI engineers at the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence (ENSIA), we noticed a common problem: learning new skills can be expensive, difficult, or limited by access to the right people. We asked ourselves, "What if there was a platform where anyone could teach a skill they excel at and learn something they desire, completely for free?" This idea sparked the creation of Swap, a platform designed to connect learners and experts across Algeria, enabling skill exchange and collaboration in a fun, accessible, and empowering way.

Our values

Accessibility

Learning should be free and easy for everyone, regardless of background or location

Collaboration

We believe in connecting people to share knowledge and grow together through mutual support.

Innovation

We strive to create creative solutions that empower users to teach, learn, and exchange skills effectively.
